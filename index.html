01 — Research focus

One question, three strands.

How can scarce, mismatched data support the integrated planning of water, energy and food? I approach it from the system down, from the satellite up, and through the data that connect the two.

A

Coupled systems modelling

Simulation–optimization of the water–energy–food nexus: holistic frameworks for the energy and food subsystems, applied to the Great Karun system and to national energy supply in a water-stressed country.

7 papers
B

Earth observation for data-scarce basins

Evapotranspiration, soil moisture, river discharge, crop patterns, bathymetry and water quality — mapped with Google Earth Engine and Python where gauges are few — plus the evaluation of satellite precipitation for drought monitoring.

8 projects · 1 paper
C

Machine learning & data integration

Fusing multi-sensor, multi-resolution and multi-temporal data; handling sparsity, outliers and uncertainty; learning-based estimation of reservoir water quality and classification of crop patterns.

03 — Projects & toolkit

Nine studies, one toolbox.

Applied work from nexus simulation to Earth observation. The remote-sensing studies are built in Google Earth Engine, several through its Python API. The thumbnails are schematic illustrations of each method — not results.

Teamwork · Flagship

Comprehensive WEF-nexus simulation–optimization

No holistic framework captured the interdependencies between the water, energy and food sectors.

Approach & impact
Approach
Built an integrated simulation model with a multidisciplinary team — one Ph.D. and three M.Sc. researchers — and developed it into simulation–optimization studies.
Impact
A dynamic framework for evaluating sustainable-development policies and resource allocation under different scenarios; the basis of several peer-reviewed papers.
  • Simulation–optimization
  • Scenario analysis
  • Policy evaluation

Earth observation · Hydrology

Evapotranspiration mapping for water-resource management

Continuous spatial data on actual evapotranspiration are missing, yet vital to nexus analysis.

Approach & impact
Approach
Implemented SEBAL and OPTRAM-ET in Google Earth Engine and ENVI, combining remote sensing with meteorological parameters.
Impact
More precise estimates of agricultural water consumption, supporting sustainable water-management scenarios.
  • GEE
  • ENVI
  • SEBAL
  • OPTRAM-ET

Earth observation · Climate

Climate–phenology–hydrology interactions

Climate and vegetation dynamics interact in complex ways, especially in data-scarce regions.

Approach & impact
Approach
Custom scripts using the Google Earth Engine API through Python to process and analyze large multi-temporal geospatial datasets.
Impact
Actionable insight into how environmental stressors shape crop patterns and yield estimates.
  • GEE
  • Python API
  • Phenology
  • Multi-temporal data

Earth observation · Uncertainty

High-resolution soil moisture under uncertainty

Mapping soil moisture across varied topography while managing data uncertainty and outliers.

Approach & impact
Approach
Implemented and calibrated OPTRAM and TOTRAM in Google Earth Engine to estimate surface soil moisture.
Impact
Robust geostatistical outputs for evaluating agricultural water demand and monitoring drought.
  • GEE
  • OPTRAM
  • TOTRAM
  • Geostatistics

Earth observation · Hydrology

River discharge in data-scarce basins

Estimating flows in ungauged or poorly gauged river basins.

Approach & impact
Approach
Automated Google Earth Engine workflows applying the CM/CMW method — an analysis of dry and wet pixels — to track discharge variations.
Impact
A reliable alternative for continuous river monitoring using remote sensing alone.
  • GEE
  • CM / CMW method
  • Dry & wet pixels

Earth observation · Agriculture

Crop-pattern classification and rice mapping

Approach & impact
Approach
Processed multi-temporal satellite imagery in Google Earth Engine and built classification models to map crop patterns, with a focus on rice.
Impact
High-resolution crop-distribution maps that support sustainable agricultural planning and precision farming.
  • GEE
  • Classification
  • Multi-temporal imagery
  • Rice

Earth observation · Water quality

Bathymetry and chlorophyll from space

Traditional monitoring of shallow waters is costly and spatially limited.

Approach & impact
Approach
Used multispectral data in Google Earth Engine to model bathymetry (0–15 m, Case-1 waters) and chlorophyll concentration together.
Impact
Cost-effective, large-scale monitoring of aquatic ecosystem health and physical conditions.
  • GEE
  • Multispectral
  • Bathymetry 0–15 m
  • Chlorophyll

Earth observation · Agriculture

Agricultural yield estimation
